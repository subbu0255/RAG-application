FAQ: Online Store

Q: What are your customer support hours?
A: Customer support is available Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST.

Q: How long does delivery take?
A: Standard delivery usually takes 3 to 5 business days after the order is shipped.

Q: Do you offer express delivery?
A: Yes. Express delivery is available for eligible locations and usually takes 1 to 2 business days.

Q: How can I track my order?
A: After your order is shipped, you will receive a tracking link by email. You can use that link to see the current delivery status.

Q: What is the return window?
A: Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery if they meet the return conditions.

Q: How do I request a return?
A: Contact customer support with your order number and the reason for the return. The support team will provide the next steps.

Q: When will I receive my refund?
A: Approved refunds are normally processed within 5 to 7 business days after the returned item is received and inspected.

Q: What payment methods are accepted?
A: We accept major credit cards, debit cards, and UPI.

Q: Can I cancel an order?
A: You can request cancellation before the order is shipped. Once an order has shipped, cancellation may no longer be possible.

Q: Do you ship internationally?
A: International shipping is not currently available.

Q: How can I contact customer support?
A: You can contact customer support through the support email or the contact form listed on the company website.
